How Backlink Vault Works

Three simple steps to supercharge your backlink strategy

🔍

1. Submit or Discover Links

Paste a URL, upload a .csv/.xlsx file, or let the community-sourced vault show you hundreds of pre-audited backlink opportunities organized by niche.

🤖

2. Bot Automatically Audits

Our Tiny Bot inspector fetches each link, checks HTTP status, extracts rel attributes (DoFollow/NoFollow), calculates Domain Authority (0–100), and assigns a Backlink Value Score.

📊

3. Track Your Progress

Use the Personal Backlink Tracker to log your own built backlinks per project. Track status (Live, Indexed, Pending), DA scores, anchor text, and notes all in one place.

Smart Acquisition Categorization

The bot automatically classifies every backlink opportunity by how easy it is to acquire

Easy Do-Follow

Open sites, forum profiles, directory submissions, and other platforms where you can add a DoFollow link with minimal friction.

Outreach / Guest Post

Sites with guest post guidelines or editorial requirements. Needs outreach emails and quality article submissions.

Paid / Sponsored

Sites that charge for sponsored posts or editorial placements. Clear monetary transaction required for link acquisition.

Directory / Profile

Business directories, niche web directories, and professional profile platforms that accept free or paid listings.
